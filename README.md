## ⚡ What I Do

I bridge the gap between engineering autonomous software agents and high-retention video post-production. Most developers don't understand narrative pacing and visual marketing; most video editors don't build software or automate complex APIs. I combine both domains to help founders showcase and scale their products.

- **Autonomous AI Agents & Automations:** Custom LLM reasoning pipelines, tool-calling frameworks, n8n/Make workflows, and REST API architectures.
- **SaaS Tech Video Production:** Product walkthroughs, feature launches, interactive UI demos, and commercial promo assets cut in Adobe Premiere Pro & After Effects.
- **Modern System Environments:** Development tooling, transcoding engines, and system-level pipelines configured on Arch Linux.
